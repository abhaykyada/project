QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MindMate.

What is MindMate? +

MindMate is an anonymous AI query platform for mental and sexual wellbeing. You can ask questions anytime and receive thoughtful,private answers without judgment.

Is my data safe? +

Your privacy is a core part of the platform. The service is designed to minimize personally identifying information and keep conversations private.

Can I ask questions about sexual health? +

Yes. You can privately ask about sexual wellness, intimacy, relationships and general sexual health concerns.

Do I need to use my real name? +

No. The platform is designed around anonymous conversations and does not require your real name for asking questions.

How does the payment work? +

The planned model uses a wallet system. You add money to your wallet and pay for the questions you use instead of paying a monthly subscription.

Will real doctors be available? +

Verified professionals such as physiotherapists, gynecologists, sexologists and mental health experts are planned for a future version. Appointments and consultation charges may apply.

Who can use MindMate? +

The planned service is intended for users aged 16 and above. Users should seek appropriate professional or emergency help when a situation requires it.
